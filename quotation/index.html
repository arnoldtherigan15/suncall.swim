What you get

Design-your-own bikini, with checkout

  • Deluxe, Premium or a la carte
  • Bottoms (Capri, Ivy, Io, Isla, Luma), tops (Rosy, Sunny, Daisy, Honey) or one-piece, sizes S to XL
  • All your fabrics, and you can add new ones yourself
  • Ways to wear the top, then beads, letter beads, patches, string colours, charms, stainless pieces, buttons, resin rings and rhinestones
  • Drag beads, patches and rhinestones into place. Charms, stainless pieces and buttons sit in their set places
  • Live preview and a running price, with package allowances counted for you

Stock, orders and customers

  • Stock number for each fabric and countable accessory, going down with every paid order
  • Sold-out fabrics and accessories disappear from the builder, with a low-stock email
  • Every order arrives with the full spec and a placement picture, ready to print for production
  • Order history, Excel export, refunds and discount codes
  • Customer accounts with past orders

Pages

  • Home, Design your own bikini, Inspo gallery, Packages, Accessories
  • Private Event page with a button that opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message
  • Wholesale enquiry
  • Cart, checkout and customer account
  • Size guide, shipping, returns and contact

Payments and messages

  • Card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay) through your Stripe
  • Automatic order emails from Shopify: confirmation and shipping
  • WhatsApp chat button, and you can message any customer yourself from the order page
  • Shipping rates and email set up

Not in this option: online booking for private sessions, QRIS, bank transfer and e-wallets, and automatic WhatsApp messages. Each can be added later as a separate small project.
