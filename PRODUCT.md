## Product Purpose
Suncall Swim is a custom bikini brand. The site lets a customer build her own set step by step, see it update as she chooses, and buy it. Success means a finished build lands in the bag, and every order reaches production with the full spec.

## Positioning
Every piece is assembled to order from the customer's own choices: cut, size, fabric colour, tie style, hardware and beads, including letter beads that spell a name.

## Operating Context
Builder flow from the client mockup: Bottoms (cut, size S–XL, colour) → Top (cut, size, colour) or One piece → Tie style → Hardware accessories (rings, shells, charms) → Beads (regular and letter beads) → Add to bag. Supporting pages: Designs gallery, The process, Shop, Accessories, Book a private session, Wholesale enquiry.
