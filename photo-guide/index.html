How to shoot

  • Lay each piece flat and shoot straight from above, with the camera on a tripod.
  • Don't move the camera or change the zoom during the whole shoot.
  • Plain light grey background, soft even light, no shadows.
  • Top and bottom photographed separately. Same size (M) and same layout for every fabric.
  • Plain pieces only: no beads, charms, tags, hands or props.
  • Colours must match the real fabric. No filters, and no cropping.

What to photograph

  • Bottoms (Capri, Ivy, Io, Isla, Luma) in each fabric
  • Tops (Rosy, Sunny, Daisy, Honey) in each fabric
  • One-piece in each of its fabrics
  • Fabric swatches: a close-up of each fabric
  • String colours: one photo of each
  • Accessories: one of each bead, patch, charm, button, ring, rhinestone, and every letter bead. Shot from the same height as the bikinis, so their size matches.
  • Optional: lifestyle and model photos for the homepage and Inspo page
