Photo guide for the bikini builder

The live preview in the builder is made by stacking your product photos on top of each other: a top, a bottom, then the accessories the customer drags into place. For that to look right, every photo has to be taken the same way, from the same spot, in the same light. This guide is for you and your photographer.

The setup

Set this up once and leave it in place for the whole shoot. If anything moves between photos, the pieces will not line up in the preview.

1

Camera straight overhead

Pointing straight down at 90°, on a tripod with an overhead arm or a copy stand. A phone is fine if it is locked in a holder and never moved.

2

Fixed height and zoom

Pick one height and one zoom that fit the largest piece (the one-piece), then never change them. Accessories are shot at this same height.

3

Tape marks on the table

Mark the frame corners and a centre cross with tape, so every piece goes in exactly the same place.

Background

  • Plain light grey paper or board, matte, no texture or creases.
  • The same background for every photo, garments and accessories.
  • Light grey works for all your fabrics, including Butter Yellow and Khaki, and makes cutting out the pieces clean.

Light

  • Soft, even light with no hard shadows. Two softboxes at 45°, or big window daylight with a diffuser.
  • Room lights off, so colours are not mixed.
  • If shooting by a window, finish in one session. Light changes through the day.

Camera settings (manual)

  • Manual exposure and a fixed white balance. Auto settings change between fabrics.
  • Aperture around f/8 so the whole piece is sharp.
  • Take one photo of a grey card at the start of each session.

Colour check

  • Customers choose by colour, so the photo must match the real fabric.
  • Compare the first shot of each fabric on screen with the fabric in daylight before carrying on.
  • Colour correction is fine. No filters or style edits.

Laying out each piece

Each shape is laid out the same way in every fabric. The easiest way: style the first fabric carefully, take a phone photo of it as your reference, then copy that layout for every other fabric.

Every piece

  • One size for everything. Size M is best.
  • Steamed, no creases, lint or loose threads.
  • No labels, tags, hangers, pins or hands in the shot.
  • Centred on the cross, inside the frame area.

Tops, bottoms and one-piece

  • Top and bottom are photographed separately, never together. The builder combines them.
  • Strings laid out in the same position and length for every fabric, flat and symmetrical.
  • Plain strings, no accessories, beads or charms on them. Those are added in the builder.
  • One-piece: same idea, centred, in each of its fabrics.

Accessories

  • Same camera height as the bikinis. This keeps every charm and bead at its true size next to the bikini in the preview.
  • One of each style. Several in one photo is fine if they have at least 2 cm of space between them.
  • Front side up, as they will sit on the bikini.

Do and don't

Do

  • Keep the camera, height, zoom and light fixed all shoot
  • Use the same size (M) and the same layout for each shape
  • Check colours against the real fabric
  • Leave empty space around the piece
  • Send the full, uncropped photos

Don't

  • Move the tripod or zoom between photos
  • Use auto white balance or auto exposure
  • Shoot at an angle, on a model or a mannequin
  • Add props, shadows, filters or backgrounds
  • Crop or remove the background yourself. I do that.

Optional: lifestyle photos

These are separate from the builder shots and can be taken any way you like. They are used on the homepage, the Inspo gallery, the packages and the private event page.

  • Model shots, beach, details, a private session in the store
  • Some portrait (4:5) and some landscape (3:2)
  • A few with empty space on one side for text

Files

  • JPG at full size, at least 3000 px on the long side. RAW files too if the photographer has them.
  • Name each file by what it shows: bottom-capri-leopard.jpg, top-rosy-seablue.jpg, onepiece-magenta.jpg, swatch-khaki.jpg, charm-shell.jpg, letter-a.jpg
  • Shared in one Google Drive folder.
