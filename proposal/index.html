How it works

A custom order

  1. Customer designs her bikiniPackage, shape, size, fabric, how to wear it, accessories. The price updates as she goes.
  2. Pays at checkoutQRIS, virtual account, e-wallet or card. Fabric and accessory stock goes down.
  3. Confirmation WhatsAppShe gets an order confirmation by WhatsApp and email.
  4. You make itPrint the production sheet and move the order through cutting, sewing and finishing.
  5. Shipped WhatsAppAdd the tracking number. The customer is notified automatically.

A private session booking

  1. Customer picks a dateThe calendar only shows free slots. She chooses the time and group size.
  2. Pays the depositThe date is locked for her once the deposit is paid.
  3. Confirmation WhatsAppBooking details by WhatsApp and email. It appears in your booking calendar.
  4. Reminder and balance WhatsAppBefore the session she receives a reminder with a link to pay the balance. She can also pay in the store.
  5. Session dayMark it as completed and fully paid. Every booking keeps its full payment history.

Deposit amount, how many days before the session the balance is due, and your cancellation policy are your choice. We set them together in week 1, and you can change them later yourself.
