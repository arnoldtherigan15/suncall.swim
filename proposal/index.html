Why the preview is built in layers

Photographing every possible combination is impossible. Instead, each piece is shot once per colour on a transparent background, from the same angle. The website stacks the layers you pick, so any combination shows instantly.

How the weeks go on Signature

  1. Week 1Discovery and setup. We confirm the product range, pricing rules and photo plan. I set up the store and the builder data structure.
  2. Week 2Design. Desktop and mobile designs based on your mockup. Two rounds of changes.
  3. Weeks 3–4Build. Pages, builder, preview layers, cart and checkout. You get a private preview link to follow along.
  4. Week 5Content and testing. Your photos and text go in. We test real orders on phone and desktop.
  5. Week 6Launch. Go live on your domain, plus a short video guide on managing orders and adding colours.

The timeline depends on receiving product photos by the end of week 2. Photos are usually what holds a launch back.

  1. Where are your customers, and which currencies and payment methods do they use?
  2. Does the price change by cut, colour or accessory, or is it fixed per piece?
  3. Can tops and bottoms be different sizes and colours? Can people buy just one piece?
  4. How are letter beads ordered: free text, or a limited set of letters?

  6. How do private sessions work today: dates, group size, deposit?
  7. What does a wholesale buyer need: a form only, or a price list and ordering?
  8. Do you already sell anywhere, such as Instagram or Shopify?
  9. Is there a launch date you are working towards?
